## 🚀 3626. Find Stores with Inventory Imbalance

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to identify stores where the **most expensive product has lower stock than the cheapest product**.

For each store, we need to:

- Find the **most expensive product** and its quantity.
- Find the **cheapest product** and its quantity.
- Keep only stores where the cheapest product has a higher quantity than the most expensive product.
- Calculate the **imbalance ratio** as:

> **cheapest_quantity / most_expensive_quantity**

Only stores with **at least 3 different products** are considered.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH product_counts AS (
    SELECT store_id, COUNT(DISTINCT product_name) as product_count
    FROM inventory
    GROUP BY store_id),
ranked AS (
    SELECT store_id, product_name, quantity, price,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY store_id ORDER BY price DESC, inventory_id ASC) AS rank_expensive,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY store_id ORDER BY price ASC, inventory_id ASC) AS rank_cheap
    FROM inventory)
SELECT s.store_id, s.store_name, s.location, 
e.product_name as most_exp_product, c.product_name as cheapest_product,
ROUND(c.quantity/e.quantity,2) as imbalance_ratio  FROM stores s
INNER JOIN ranked e on e.store_id = s.store_id AND e.rank_expensive = 1
INNER JOIN ranked c on c.store_id = s.store_id AND c.rank_cheap = 1
INNER JOIN product_counts pc on pc.store_id = s.store_id
WHERE c.quantity > e.quantity AND pc.product_count >= 3
ORDER BY imbalance_ratio DESC, s.store_name
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. The `product_counts` CTE counts the number of different products in each store.
2. The `ranked` CTE uses `ROW_NUMBER()` to identify the most expensive and cheapest products for every store.
3. Join the `ranked` CTE twice to retrieve both products.
4. Keep only stores where:
   - The cheapest product has a higher quantity.
   - The store has at least **3 different products**.
5. Calculate the imbalance ratio and sort the result.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)`

- The window functions sort the products within each store.
- The remaining joins and filtering are linear.

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

- Used to store the intermediate CTE results.
