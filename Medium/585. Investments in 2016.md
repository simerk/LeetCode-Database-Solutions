## 🚀 585. Investments in 2016

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to report the sum of all total investment values in 2016 tiv_2016, for all policyholders who:

- have the same `tiv_2015` value as one or more other policyholders, and
- are not located in the same city as any other policyholder (i.e., the (`lat, lon`) attribute pairs must be unique).

Round `tiv_2016` to **two decimal places**.

---

### 📝 Example

#### Input

Insurance table:

| pid | tiv_2015 | tiv_2016 | lat | lon |
|-----|----------|----------|-----|-----|
| 1   | 10       | 5        | 10  | 10  |
| 2   | 20       | 20       | 20  | 20  |
| 3   | 10       | 30       | 20  | 20  |
| 4   | 10       | 40       | 40  | 40  |

#### Output

| tiv_2016 |
|----------|
| 45.00    |

#### Explanation

The first record in the table, like the last record, meets both of the two criteria.
The tiv_2015 value 10 is the same as the third and fourth records, and its location is unique.

The second record does not meet any of the two criteria. Its tiv_2015 is not like any other policyholders and its location is the same as the third record, which makes the third record fail, too.

So, the result is the sum of tiv_2016 of the first and last record, which is 45.

---

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to find the **total investment value in 2016 (`tiv_2016`)** for policyholders who satisfy **both** of the following conditions:

- Their `tiv_2015` value is **shared with at least one other policyholder**.
- Their location (`lat`, `lon`) is **unique**.

We can identify these conditions using two subqueries with `GROUP BY` and `HAVING`, then sum the qualifying `tiv_2016` values.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT ROUND(SUM(tiv_2016), 2) AS tiv_2016
FROM Insurance
WHERE tiv_2015 IN (
    SELECT tiv_2015
    FROM Insurance
    GROUP BY tiv_2015
    HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
)
AND (lat, lon) IN (
    SELECT lat, lon
    FROM Insurance
    GROUP BY lat, lon
    HAVING COUNT(*) = 1
);
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Find all `tiv_2015` values that appear more than once.
2. Find all unique `(lat, lon)` location pairs.
3. Filter the `Insurance` table to keep only records that satisfy both conditions.
4. Sum the corresponding `tiv_2016` values.
5. Round the final result to **2 decimal places**.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(multiple grouping operations with linear scans)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores grouped values for `tiv_2015` and `(lat, lon)` combinations)*
