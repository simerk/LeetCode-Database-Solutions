## 🚀 1364. Number of Trusted Contacts of a Customer 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to find the following for each `invoice_id`:

- `customer_name`: The name of the customer the invoice is related to.
- `price`: The price of the invoice.
- `contacts_cnt`: The number of contacts related to the customer.
- `trusted_contacts_cnt`: The number of contacts related to the customer and at the same time they are customers to the shop. (i.e their email exists in the `Customers` table.)

Return the result table ordered by `invoice_id`.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Contacts table:                                        Invoices table:                     Customers table:   
+-------------+--------------+--------------------+    +------------+-------+---------+    +-------------+---------------+--------------------+
| user_id     | contact_name | contact_email      |    | invoice_id | price | user_id |    | customer_id | customer_name | email              |
+-------------+--------------+--------------------+    +------------+-------+---------+    +-------------+---------------+--------------------+
| 1           | Bob          | bob@leetcode.com   |    | 77         | 100   | 1       |    | 1           | Alice         | alice@leetcode.com |
| 1           | John         | john@leetcode.com  |    | 88         | 200   | 1       |    | 2           | Bob           | bob@leetcode.com   |
| 1           | Jal          | jal@leetcode.com   |    | 99         | 300   | 2       |    | 13          | John          | john@leetcode.com  |
| 2           | Omar         | omar@leetcode.com  |    | 66         | 400   | 2       |    | 6           | Alex          | alex@leetcode.com  |
| 2           | Meir         | meir@leetcode.com  |    | 55         | 500   | 13      |    +-------------+---------------+--------------------+
| 6           | Alice        | alice@leetcode.com |    | 44         | 60    | 6       |
+-------------+--------------+--------------------+    +------------+-------+---------+
Output: 
+------------+---------------+-------+--------------+----------------------+
| invoice_id | customer_name | price | contacts_cnt | trusted_contacts_cnt |
+------------+---------------+-------+--------------+----------------------+
| 44         | Alex          | 60    | 1            | 1                    |
| 55         | John          | 500   | 0            | 0                    |
| 66         | Bob           | 400   | 2            | 0                    |
| 77         | Alice         | 100   | 3            | 2                    |
| 88         | Alice         | 200   | 3            | 2                    |
| 99         | Bob           | 300   | 2            | 0                    |
+------------+---------------+-------+--------------+----------------------+
Explanation: 
Alice has three contacts, two of them are trusted contacts (Bob and John).
Bob has two contacts, none of them is a trusted contact.
Alex has one contact and it is a trusted contact (Alice).
John doesn't have any contacts.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

First count the total contacts and trusted contacts for each customer. A trusted contact is one whose email exists in the `Customers` table. Then join these counts with `Invoices`.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH contact_counts AS (
    SELECT c.user_id, COUNT(*) AS contacts_cnt,
    SUM(CASE WHEN cu.email IS NOT NULL THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS trusted_contacts_cnt
    FROM Contacts c
    LEFT JOIN Customers cu ON c.contact_email = cu.email
    GROUP BY c.user_id
)
SELECT i.invoice_id, c.customer_name, i.price,
COALESCE(cc.contacts_cnt, 0) AS contacts_cnt,
COALESCE(cc.trusted_contacts_cnt, 0) AS trusted_contacts_cnt
FROM Invoices i
JOIN Customers c ON i.user_id = c.customer_id
LEFT JOIN contact_counts cc ON i.user_id = cc.user_id
ORDER BY i.invoice_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Join each contact's email with `Customers.email` to identify trusted contacts.
2. Count all contacts and trusted contacts for each customer.
3. Join these counts with `Invoices` and `Customers`.
4. Use `COALESCE()` to return `0` for customers with no contacts.
5. Order the result by `invoice_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(C + I + K)` *(processes customers, invoices, and contacts)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(C)` *(stores contact counts for each customer)*
