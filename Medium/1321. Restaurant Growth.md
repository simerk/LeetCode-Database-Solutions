## 🚀 1321. Restaurant Growth

### 📖 Problem

You are the restaurant owner and you want to analyze a possible expansion (there will be at least one customer every day).

Compute the moving average of how much the customer paid in a seven days window (i.e., current day + 6 days before). `average_amount` should be **rounded to two decimal places**.

Return the result table ordered by `visited_on` in **ascending order**.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Customer table:
+-------------+--------------+--------------+-------------+
| customer_id | name         | visited_on   | amount      |
+-------------+--------------+--------------+-------------+
| 1           | Jhon         | 2019-01-01   | 100         |
| 2           | Daniel       | 2019-01-02   | 110         |
| 3           | Jade         | 2019-01-03   | 120         |
| 4           | Khaled       | 2019-01-04   | 130         |
| 5           | Winston      | 2019-01-05   | 110         | 
| 6           | Elvis        | 2019-01-06   | 140         | 
| 7           | Anna         | 2019-01-07   | 150         |
| 8           | Maria        | 2019-01-08   | 80          |
| 9           | Jaze         | 2019-01-09   | 110         | 
| 1           | Jhon         | 2019-01-10   | 130         | 
| 3           | Jade         | 2019-01-10   | 150         | 
+-------------+--------------+--------------+-------------+
Output: 
+--------------+--------------+----------------+
| visited_on   | amount       | average_amount |
+--------------+--------------+----------------+
| 2019-01-07   | 860          | 122.86         |
| 2019-01-08   | 840          | 120            |
| 2019-01-09   | 840          | 120            |
| 2019-01-10   | 1000         | 142.86         |
+--------------+--------------+----------------+
Explanation: 
1st moving average from 2019-01-01 to 2019-01-07 has an average_amount of (100 + 110 + 120 + 130 + 110 + 140 + 150)/7 = 122.86
2nd moving average from 2019-01-02 to 2019-01-08 has an average_amount of (110 + 120 + 130 + 110 + 140 + 150 + 80)/7 = 120
3rd moving average from 2019-01-03 to 2019-01-09 has an average_amount of (120 + 130 + 110 + 140 + 150 + 80 + 110)/7 = 120
4th moving average from 2019-01-04 to 2019-01-10 has an average_amount of (130 + 110 + 140 + 150 + 80 + 110 + 130 + 150)/7 = 142.86
```
---


### 💡 Idea

The goal is to calculate the **7-day moving average** of the restaurant's revenue.

For each day (starting from the 7th day), we need to report:

- The current date.
- The total revenue over the **current day and the previous 6 days**.
- The average daily revenue over those 7 days.

We first calculate the total revenue for each day, then use window functions to compute the rolling 7-day sum and average.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT visited_on, amount, ROUND(amount / 7, 2) AS average_amount
FROM (
    SELECT visited_on,
    SUM(amount) OVER (ORDER BY visited_on ROWS BETWEEN 6 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW) AS amount,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY visited_on) AS rn
    FROM (
        SELECT visited_on,
        SUM(amount) AS amount
        FROM Customer
        GROUP BY visited_on
    ) t
) s
WHERE rn >= 7;
```
---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Group the `Customer` table by `visited_on` to calculate the total revenue for each day.
2. Use `SUM() OVER` with a window frame to calculate the rolling 7-day revenue.
3. Use `ROW_NUMBER()` to number the days in chronological order.
4. Exclude the first six days since a complete 7-day window is not available.
5. Divide the 7-day total by `7` to obtain the average daily revenue.
6. Round the average to **2 decimal places**.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)` *(sorting required for window functions)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores daily totals and running window values)*
