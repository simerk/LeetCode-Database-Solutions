## 🚀 1715. Count Apples and Oranges 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to count the number of apples and oranges in all the boxes. If a box contains a chest, you should also include the number of apples and oranges it has.

---

### 📝 Example

```text
Input: 
Boxes table:                                          Chests table:
+--------+----------+-------------+--------------+    +----------+-------------+--------------+
| box_id | chest_id | apple_count | orange_count |    | chest_id | apple_count | orange_count |
+--------+----------+-------------+--------------+    +----------+-------------+--------------+
| 2      | null     | 6           | 15           |    | 6        | 5           | 6            |
| 18     | 14       | 4           | 15           |    | 14       | 20          | 10           |
| 19     | 3        | 8           | 4            |    | 2        | 8           | 8            |
| 12     | 2        | 19          | 20           |    | 3        | 19          | 4            |
| 20     | 6        | 12          | 9            |    | 16       | 19          | 19           |
| 8      | 6        | 9           | 9            |    +----------+-------------+--------------+
| 3      | 14       | 16          | 7            |
+--------+----------+-------------+--------------+
Output: 
+-------------+--------------+
| apple_count | orange_count |
+-------------+--------------+
| 151         | 123          |
+-------------+--------------+
Explanation: 
box 2 has 6 apples and 15 oranges.
box 18 has 4 + 20 (from the chest) = 24 apples and 15 + 10 (from the chest) = 25 oranges.
box 19 has 8 + 19 (from the chest) = 27 apples and 4 + 4 (from the chest) = 8 oranges.
box 12 has 19 + 8 (from the chest) = 27 apples and 20 + 8 (from the chest) = 28 oranges.
box 20 has 12 + 5 (from the chest) = 17 apples and 9 + 6 (from the chest) = 15 oranges.
box 8 has 9 + 5 (from the chest) = 14 apples and 9 + 6 (from the chest) = 15 oranges.
box 3 has 16 + 20 (from the chest) = 36 apples and 7 + 10 (from the chest) = 17 oranges.
Total number of apples = 6 + 24 + 27 + 27 + 17 + 14 + 36 = 151
Total number of oranges = 15 + 25 + 8 + 28 + 15 + 15 + 17 = 123
```
---

### 💡 Idea

Use a `LEFT JOIN` to attach each chest to its box. Then add the fruits directly in the box to the fruits inside its chest.

`COALESCE()` handles boxes that do not contain a chest.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT
    SUM(b.apple_count + COALESCE(c.apple_count, 0)) AS apple_count,
    SUM(b.orange_count + COALESCE(c.orange_count, 0)) AS orange_count
FROM Boxes b
LEFT JOIN Chests c ON b.chest_id = c.chest_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. `LEFT JOIN` matches each box with its chest, if it has one.
2. `COALESCE()` converts missing chest counts to `0`.
3. Add the box and chest apple counts together.
4. Add the box and chest orange counts together.
5. `SUM()` calculates the total apples and oranges across all boxes.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(processes all boxes and their matching chests)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(for the join operation)*
