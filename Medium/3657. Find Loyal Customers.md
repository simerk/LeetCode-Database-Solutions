## 🚀 3657. Find Loyal Customers

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to identify **loyal customers**.

A customer is considered loyal if they:

- Made at least **3 purchase transactions**.
- Have been active for at least **30 days**.
- Have a **refund rate less than 20%**.

We group the transactions by customer and use aggregate functions to check all three conditions.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT customer_id FROM customer_transactions
GROUP BY customer_id
HAVING SUM(transaction_type = 'purchase') >= 3
AND DATEDIFF(MAX(transaction_date), MIN(transaction_date)) >= 30
AND AVG(transaction_type = 'refund') * 100 < 20
ORDER BY customer_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Group all transactions by `customer_id`.
2. `SUM(transaction_type = 'purchase')` counts the number of purchase transactions. In MySQL, the expression returns `1` for purchases and `0` otherwise.
3. `DATEDIFF(MAX(transaction_date), MIN(transaction_date))` calculates how many days the customer has been active.
4. `AVG(transaction_type = 'refund') * 100` calculates the refund percentage by averaging the boolean expression (`1` for refunds and `0` otherwise).
5. Keep only customers who:
   - Have at least **3 purchases**.
   - Have been active for at least **30 days**.
   - Have a refund rate less than **20%**.
6. Sort the result by `customer_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)`

- `n` = number of rows in the `customer_transactions` table.

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

- Used for grouping the transactions by customer.
