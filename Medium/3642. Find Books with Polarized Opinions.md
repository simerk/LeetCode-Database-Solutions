## 🚀 3642. Find Books with Polarized Opinions

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to find books that have **polarized opinions** - books that receive both very high ratings and very low ratings from different readers.

- A book has polarized opinions if it has `at least one rating ≥ 4` and `at least one rating ≤ 2`
- Only consider books that have **at least** `5` **reading sessions**
- Calculate the **rating spread** as (`highest_rating - lowest_rating`)
- Calculate the **polarization score** as the number of extreme ratings (`ratings ≤ 2 or ≥ 4`) divided by total sessions
- **Only include** books where `polarization score ≥ 0.6` (at least `60%` extreme ratings)
  
Return the result table ordered by `polarization score` in **descending** order, then by `title` in **descending** order.
The **polarization score** should be rounded to 2 decimal places.

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to find books that have **polarized opinions**.

A book is considered polarized if:

- It has at least one **high rating (≥ 4)** and at least one **low rating (≤ 2)**.
- It has at least **5 reading sessions**.
- At least **60%** of its ratings are extreme (≤ 2 or ≥ 4).

We first calculate the required statistics for each book in a CTE and then filter the books that satisfy all the conditions.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH ratings AS (
    SELECT book_id,
    COUNT(DISTINCT session_id) AS total_sessions,
    MAX(session_rating) AS highest_rating,
    MIN(session_rating) AS lowest_rating,
    SUM(CASE WHEN session_rating <= 2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS low_ratings,
    SUM(CASE WHEN session_rating >= 4 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS high_ratings,
    SUM(CASE WHEN session_rating <= 2 OR session_rating >= 4 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS extreme_ratings
    FROM reading_sessions
    GROUP BY book_id
)
SELECT b.book_id, b.title, b.author, b.genre, b.pages,
r.highest_rating-r.lowest_rating rating_spread,
ROUND(r.extreme_ratings / r.total_sessions, 2) AS polarization_score
FROM books b
INNER JOIN ratings r ON b.book_id = r.book_id
WHERE total_sessions >= 5 AND high_ratings > 0 AND low_ratings > 0
HAVING polarization_score >= 0.6
ORDER BY polarization_score DESC, title DESC;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. The `ratings` CTE calculates the statistics for each book:
   - Total reading sessions.
   - Highest and lowest ratings.
   - Number of low ratings (≤ 2).
   - Number of high ratings (≥ 4).
   - Number of extreme ratings (≤ 2 or ≥ 4).
2. Join the CTE with the `books` table.
3. Calculate:
   - **Rating Spread** = Highest Rating − Lowest Rating.
   - **Polarization Score** = Extreme Ratings ÷ Total Sessions.
4. Keep only books that:
   - Have at least **5 sessions**.
   - Have both high and low ratings.
   - Have a polarization score of at least **0.6**.
5. Sort the result by polarization score (descending) and title (descending).

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)`

- `n` = number of rows in the `reading_sessions` table.

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

- Used to store the aggregated statistics for each book.
