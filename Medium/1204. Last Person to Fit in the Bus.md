## 🚀 1204. Last Person to Fit in the Bus

### 📖 Problem

There is a queue of people waiting to board a bus. However, the bus has a weight limit of `1000` kilograms, so there may be some people who cannot board.

Write a solution to find the `person_name` of the last person that can fit on the bus without exceeding the weight limit. The test cases are generated such that the first person does not exceed the weight limit.

**Note:** that only one person can board the bus at any given turn.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Queue table:
+-----------+-------------+--------+------+
| person_id | person_name | weight | turn |
+-----------+-------------+--------+------+
| 5         | Alice       | 250    | 1    |
| 4         | Bob         | 175    | 5    |
| 3         | Alex        | 350    | 2    |
| 6         | John Cena   | 400    | 3    |
| 1         | Winston     | 500    | 6    |
| 2         | Marie       | 200    | 4    |
+-----------+-------------+--------+------+
Output: 
+-------------+
| person_name |
+-------------+
| John Cena   |
+-------------+
Explanation: The folowing table is ordered by the turn for simplicity.
+------+----+-----------+--------+--------------+
| Turn | ID | Name      | Weight | Total Weight |
+------+----+-----------+--------+--------------+
| 1    | 5  | Alice     | 250    | 250          |
| 2    | 3  | Alex      | 350    | 600          |
| 3    | 6  | John Cena | 400    | 1000         | (last person to board)
| 4    | 2  | Marie     | 200    | 1200         | (cannot board)
| 5    | 4  | Bob       | 175    | ___          |
| 6    | 1  | Winston   | 500    | ___          |
+------+----+-----------+--------+--------------+
```
---


### 💡 Idea

The goal is to find the **last person who can board the bus** without exceeding the weight limit of **1000 kg**.

We calculate the **running total** (cumulative sum) of the passengers' weights in the order of their turn. The last person whose cumulative weight is less than or equal to **1000** is the answer.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT person_name
FROM (
    SELECT person_name, turn,
    SUM(weight) OVER (ORDER BY turn) AS total_weight
    FROM Queue
) t
WHERE total_weight <= 1000
ORDER BY total_weight DESC
LIMIT 1;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Sort the passengers by their `turn`.
2. Use a window function to calculate the cumulative (`running`) sum of weights.
3. Keep only the passengers whose cumulative weight is **≤ 1000**.
4. Among them, the passenger with the largest cumulative weight is the last one who can board.
5. Return that passenger's name.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)` *(sorting by `turn` for the window function)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores the running total for each passenger)*
