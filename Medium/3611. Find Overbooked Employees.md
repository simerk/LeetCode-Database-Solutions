## 🚀 3611. Find Overbooked Employees

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to find employees who are **meeting-heavy** - employees who spend more than `50%` of their working time in meetings during any given week.

- Assume a standard work week is `40` **hours**
- Calculate **total meeting hours** per employee **per week (Monday to Sunday)**
- An employee is meeting-heavy if their weekly meeting hours `> 20` hours (`50%` of `40` hours)
- Count how many weeks each employee was meeting-heavy
- **Only include** employees who were meeting-heavy for **at least** `2` **weeks**
  
Return the result table ordered by the `number of meeting-heavy weeks` in **descending** order, then by `employee name` in **ascending** order.

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to find employees who spend **more than 50% of their working time** in meetings during a week.

Since a standard work week is **40 hours**, an employee is considered **meeting-heavy** if they spend **more than 20 hours** in meetings during any week.

We first calculate the total meeting hours for each employee in every week. Then, we count how many weeks each employee was meeting-heavy and return only those employees who were meeting-heavy for **at least 2 weeks**.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH weekly_meetings AS (
    SELECT employee_id,
    YEAR(meeting_date) AS year,
    WEEK(meeting_date,1) AS week_no,
    SUM(duration_hours) AS meeting_hours
    FROM meetings
    GROUP BY employee_id, YEAR(meeting_date), WEEK(meeting_date,1)
),
heavy_weeks AS (
    SELECT employee_id,
    COUNT(*) AS meeting_heavy_weeks
    FROM weekly_meetings
    WHERE meeting_hours > 20
    GROUP BY employee_id
)
SELECT e.employee_id, e.employee_name, e.department, h.meeting_heavy_weeks
FROM heavy_weeks h
JOIN employees e ON h.employee_id = e.employee_id
WHERE h.meeting_heavy_weeks >= 2
ORDER BY meeting_heavy_weeks DESC, employee_name ASC;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. The `weekly_meetings` CTE calculates the total meeting hours for each employee in every week.
2. The `heavy_weeks` CTE counts how many weeks each employee spent **more than 20 hours** in meetings.
3. Join the result with the `employees` table to retrieve employee details.
4. Keep only employees who were meeting-heavy for at least **2 weeks**.
5. Sort the result by the number of meeting-heavy weeks and employee name.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)`

- `n` = number of rows in the `meetings` table.

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)`

- Used to store the weekly aggregated meeting hours in the CTE.
