## 🚀 1398. Customers Who Bought Products A and B but Not C 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to report the customer_id and customer_name of customers who bought products "**A**", "**B**" but did not buy the product "**C**" since we want to recommend them to purchase this product.

Return the result table **ordered** by `customer_id`.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Orders table:                                      Customers table:
+------------+--------------+---------------+      +-------------+---------------+
| order_id   | customer_id  | product_name  |      | customer_id | customer_name |
+------------+--------------+---------------+      +-------------+---------------+
| 10         |     1        |     A         |      | 1           | Daniel        |
| 20         |     1        |     B         |      | 2           | Diana         |
| 30         |     1        |     D         |      | 3           | Elizabeth     |
| 40         |     1        |     C         |      | 4           | Jhon          |
| 50         |     2        |     A         |      +-------------+---------------+
| 60         |     3        |     A         |
| 70         |     3        |     B         |
| 80         |     3        |     D         |
| 90         |     4        |     C         |
+------------+--------------+---------------+
Output: 
+-------------+---------------+
| customer_id | customer_name |
+-------------+---------------+
| 3           | Elizabeth     |
+-------------+---------------+
Explanation: Only the customer_id with id 3 bought the product A and B but not the product C.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

Group orders by customer and check whether each customer has bought **A** and **B**, while making sure they have **not bought C**.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT c.customer_id, c.customer_name
FROM Customers c
JOIN Orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.customer_name
HAVING SUM(o.product_name = 'A') > 0
   AND SUM(o.product_name = 'B') > 0
   AND SUM(o.product_name = 'C') = 0
ORDER BY c.customer_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Join customers with their orders.
2. Group all orders by customer.
3. Check that the customer bought at least one `A`.
4. Check that the customer bought at least one `B`.
5. Exclude customers who bought `C`.
6. Order the result by `customer_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(scans and groups the orders)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(c)` *(stores aggregated results for each customer)*
