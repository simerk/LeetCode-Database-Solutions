## 🚀 1677. Product's Worth Over Invoices 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution that will, for all products, return each product name with the total amount due, paid, canceled, and refunded across all invoices.

Return the result table ordered by `product_name`.

---

### 📝 Example

```text
Input: 
Invoice table:                                                     Product table:
+------------+------------+------+------+----------+----------+    +------------+-------+
| invoice_id | product_id | rest | paid | canceled | refunded |    | product_id | name  |
+------------+------------+------+------+----------+----------+    +------------+-------+
| 23         | 0          | 2    | 0    | 5        | 0        |    | 0          | ham   |
| 12         | 0          | 0    | 4    | 0        | 3        |    | 1          | bacon |
| 1          | 1          | 1    | 1    | 0        | 1        |    +------------+-------+
| 2          | 1          | 1    | 0    | 1        | 1        |
| 3          | 1          | 0    | 1    | 1        | 1        |
| 4          | 1          | 1    | 1    | 1        | 0        |
+------------+------------+------+------+----------+----------+
Output: 
+-------+------+------+----------+----------+
| name  | rest | paid | canceled | refunded |
+-------+------+------+----------+----------+
| bacon | 3    | 3    | 3        | 3        |
| ham   | 2    | 4    | 5        | 3        |
+-------+------+------+----------+----------+
Explanation: 
- The amount of money left to pay for bacon is 1 + 1 + 0 + 1 = 3
- The amount of money paid for bacon is 1 + 0 + 1 + 1 = 3
- The amount of money canceled for bacon is 0 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 3
- The amount of money refunded for bacon is 1 + 1 + 1 + 0 = 3
- The amount of money left to pay for ham is 2 + 0 = 2
- The amount of money paid for ham is 0 + 4 = 4
- The amount of money canceled for ham is 5 + 0 = 5
- The amount of money refunded for ham is 0 + 3 = 3
```
---

### 💡 Idea

Use a `LEFT JOIN` to include every product, then sum each invoice amount separately. `COALESCE()` ensures products without invoices return `0`.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT p.name,
    COALESCE(SUM(i.rest), 0) AS rest,
    COALESCE(SUM(i.paid), 0) AS paid,
    COALESCE(SUM(i.canceled), 0) AS canceled,
    COALESCE(SUM(i.refunded), 0) AS refunded
FROM Product p
LEFT JOIN Invoice i ON p.product_id = i.product_id
GROUP BY p.product_id, p.name
ORDER BY p.name;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. `LEFT JOIN` connects each product with all its invoices.
2. `SUM()` calculates the total `rest`, `paid`, `canceled`, and `refunded` amounts.
3. `COALESCE()` returns `0` for products with no invoices.
4. Group by product to produce one row per product.
5. Sort the result by `product_name`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(processes all invoice records)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(for grouping the invoice records)*
