## 🚀 1083. Sales Analysis II 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to report the buyers who have bought *S8* but not *iPhone*. Note that *S8* and *iPhone* are products presented in the `Product` table.

Return the result table in **any order**.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Product table:                                   Sales table:
+------------+--------------+------------+       +-----------+------------+----------+------------+----------+-------+
| product_id | product_name | unit_price |       | seller_id | product_id | buyer_id | sale_date  | quantity | price |
+------------+--------------+------------+       +-----------+------------+----------+------------+----------+-------+
| 1          | S8           | 1000       |       | 1         | 1          | 1        | 2019-01-21 | 2        | 2000  |
| 2          | G4           | 800        |       | 1         | 2          | 2        | 2019-02-17 | 1        | 800   |
| 3          | iPhone       | 1400       |       | 2         | 1          | 3        | 2019-06-02 | 1        | 800   |
+------------+--------------+------------+       | 3         | 3          | 3        | 2019-05-13 | 2        | 2800  |
                                                 +-----------+------------+----------+------------+----------+-------+
Output: 
+-------------+
| buyer_id    |
+-------------+
| 1           |
+-------------+
Explanation: The buyer with id 1 bought an S8 but did not buy an iPhone. The buyer with id 3 bought both.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

We need to find buyers who **bought S8 but never bought iPhone**.

We can join `Sales` with `Product` to identify the products purchased by each buyer, then group by `buyer_id` and use conditional aggregation to check both conditions.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql id="h5q2nz"
SELECT s.buyer_id FROM Sales s
JOIN Product p ON s.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY s.buyer_id
HAVING SUM(p.product_name = 'S8') > 0
   AND SUM(p.product_name = 'iPhone') = 0;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Join `Sales` with `Product` to get the product name for each sale.
2. Group the purchases by `buyer_id`.
3. Check that the buyer purchased at least one `S8`.
4. Check that the buyer purchased zero `iPhone` products.
5. Return the qualifying buyer IDs.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(scans the sales records and groups them by buyer)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(k)` *(stores aggregated information for each unique buyer)*
