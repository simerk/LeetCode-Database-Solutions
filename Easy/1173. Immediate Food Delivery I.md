## 🚀 1173. Immediate Food Delivery I 🔒

### 📖 Problem

If the customer's preferred delivery date is the same as the order date, then the order is called **immediate**; otherwise, it is called **scheduled**.

Write a solution to find the percentage of immediate orders in the table, **rounded to 2 decimal places**.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Delivery table:
+-------------+-------------+------------+-----------------------------+
| delivery_id | customer_id | order_date | customer_pref_delivery_date |
+-------------+-------------+------------+-----------------------------+
| 1           | 1           | 2019-08-01 | 2019-08-02                  |
| 2           | 5           | 2019-08-02 | 2019-08-02                  |
| 3           | 1           | 2019-08-11 | 2019-08-11                  |
| 4           | 3           | 2019-08-24 | 2019-08-26                  |
| 5           | 4           | 2019-08-21 | 2019-08-22                  |
| 6           | 2           | 2019-08-11 | 2019-08-13                  |
+-------------+-------------+------------+-----------------------------+
Output: 
+----------------------+
| immediate_percentage |
+----------------------+
| 33.33                |
+----------------------+
Explanation: The orders with delivery id 2 and 3 are immediate while the others are scheduled.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

An order is **immediate** when the `order_date` is the same as the `customer_pref_delivery_date`.

We can calculate the percentage directly using conditional aggregation, then round it to 2 decimal places.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT
    ROUND(
        AVG(order_date = customer_pref_delivery_date) * 100,
        2) AS immediate_percentage
FROM Delivery;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Compare `order_date` with `customer_pref_delivery_date`.
2. The comparison returns `1` for immediate orders and `0` otherwise.
3. `AVG()` calculates the proportion of immediate orders.
4. Multiply by `100` to convert it into a percentage.
5. Round the result to 2 decimal places.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(checks each delivery once)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(1)` *(uses only aggregate calculations)*
