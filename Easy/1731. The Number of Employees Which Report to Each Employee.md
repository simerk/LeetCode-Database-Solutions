## 🚀 1731. The Number of Employees Which Report to Each Employee

### 📖 Problem

For this problem, we will consider a **manager** an employee who has at least 1 other employee reporting to them.

Write a solution to report the ids and the names of all managers, the number of employees who report **directly** to them, and the average age of the reports rounded to the nearest integer.

Return the result table ordered by `employee_id`.

---

### 📝 Example 1
```text
Input: 
Employees table:
+-------------+---------+------------+-----+
| employee_id | name    | reports_to | age |
+-------------+---------+------------+-----+
| 9           | Hercy   | null       | 43  |
| 6           | Alice   | 9          | 41  |
| 4           | Bob     | 9          | 36  |
| 2           | Winston | null       | 37  |
+-------------+---------+------------+-----+
Output: 
+-------------+-------+---------------+-------------+
| employee_id | name  | reports_count | average_age |
+-------------+-------+---------------+-------------+
| 9           | Hercy | 2             | 39          |
+-------------+-------+---------------+-------------+
Explanation: Hercy has 2 people report directly to him, Alice and Bob.
Their average age is (41+36)/2 = 38.5, which is 39 after rounding it to the nearest integer.
```
### 📝 Example 2

```text
Input: 
Employees table:
+-------------+---------+------------+-----+ 
| employee_id | name    | reports_to | age |
|-------------|---------|------------|-----|
| 1           | Michael | null       | 45  |
| 2           | Alice   | 1          | 38  |
| 3           | Bob     | 1          | 42  |
| 4           | Charlie | 2          | 34  |

| 6           | Eve     | 3          | 37  |
| 7           | Frank   | null       | 50  |
| 8           | Grace   | null       | 48  |
+-------------+---------+------------+-----+

+-------------+---------+---------------+-------------+
| employee_id | name    | reports_count | average_age |
| ----------- | ------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| 1           | Michael | 2             | 40          |
| 2           | Alice   | 2             | 37          |
| 3           | Bob     | 1             | 37          |
+-------------+---------+---------------+-------------+
```
---

### 💡 Idea

The goal is to find, for each manager:

- The manager's employee ID.
- The manager's name.
- The number of employees who directly report to them.
- The average age of those employees (rounded to the nearest integer).

Since both managers and employees are stored in the same table, we use a **self join** to match each employee with their manager.

---

## 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT m.employee_id, m.name,
COUNT(e.employee_id) AS reports_count,
ROUND(AVG(e.age), 0) AS average_age
FROM Employees m
JOIN Employees e
ON m.employee_id = e.reports_to
GROUP BY m.employee_id
ORDER BY m.employee_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Treat the `Employees` table as two separate tables:
   - `m` → managers.
   - `e` → employees.
2. Join them where:
   - `m.employee_id = e.reports_to`.
3. Group the records by each manager.
4. Count the number of employees reporting to each manager using `COUNT()`.
5. Calculate the average age of the reporting employees using `AVG()`.
6. Round the average age to the nearest integer.
7. Sort the results by `employee_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(single self join followed by grouping)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores grouped managers)*
