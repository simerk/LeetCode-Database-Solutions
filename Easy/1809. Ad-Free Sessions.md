## 🚀 1809. Ad-Free Sessions 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to report all the sessions that did not get shown any ads.

Return the result table in **any order**.

---

### 📝 Example

```text
Input: 
Playback table:                                         Ads table:
+------------+-------------+------------+----------+    +-------+-------------+-----------+
| session_id | customer_id | start_time | end_time |    | ad_id | customer_id | timestamp |
+------------+-------------+------------+----------+    +-------+-------------+-----------+
| 1          | 1           | 1          | 5        |    | 1     | 1           | 5         |
| 2          | 1           | 15         | 23       |    | 2     | 2           | 17        |
| 3          | 2           | 10         | 12       |    | 3     | 2           | 20        |
| 4          | 2           | 17         | 28       |    +-------+-------------+-----------+
| 5          | 2           | 2          | 8        |
+------------+-------------+------------+----------+
Output: 
+------------+
| session_id |
+------------+
| 2          |
| 3          |
| 5          |
+------------+
Explanation: 
The ad with ID 1 was shown to user 1 at time 5 while they were in session 1.
The ad with ID 2 was shown to user 2 at time 17 while they were in session 4.
The ad with ID 3 was shown to user 2 at time 20 while they were in session 4.
We can see that sessions 1 and 4 had at least one ad.
Sessions 2, 3, and 5 did not have any ads, so we return them.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

A session is ad-free if there is **no ad shown to the same customer during that session**.

Use a `LEFT JOIN` to match ads based on the customer and whether the ad timestamp falls within the session. Then keep only sessions with no matching ad.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT p.session_id
FROM Playback p
LEFT JOIN Ads a
    ON p.customer_id = a.customer_id
    AND a.timestamp BETWEEN p.start_time AND p.end_time
WHERE a.ad_id IS NULL;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Match each playback session with ads belonging to the same customer.
2. `BETWEEN` checks whether the ad was shown during the session.
3. `LEFT JOIN` keeps sessions even when no ad matches.
4. `WHERE a.ad_id IS NULL` selects only sessions with no ads.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n × m)` *(compares playback sessions with ads during the join)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(for the joined result)*
