## 🚀 1069. Product Sales Analysis II 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution that reports the total quantity sold for every product id.

Return the resulting table in **any order**.

---

### 📝 Example

```text
Input: 
Sales table:                                            Product table:
+---------+------------+------+----------+-------+      +------------+--------------+
| sale_id | product_id | year | quantity | price |      | product_id | product_name |
+---------+------------+------+----------+-------+      +------------+--------------+
| 1       | 100        | 2008 | 10       | 5000  |      | 100        | Nokia        |
| 2       | 100        | 2009 | 12       | 5000  |      | 200        | Apple        |
| 7       | 200        | 2011 | 15       | 9000  |      | 300        | Samsung      |
+---------+------------+------+----------+-------+      +------------+--------------+
Output: 
+--------------+----------------+
| product_id   | total_quantity |
+--------------+----------------+
| 100          | 22             |
| 200          | 15             |
+--------------+----------------+
```
---

### 💡 Idea

We need to find the **total quantity sold for each product**.

Since the `Sales` table contains the quantity for every sale, we can simply group the records by `product_id` and use `SUM()` to calculate the total quantity.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT product_id, SUM(quantity) AS total_quantity
FROM Sales
GROUP BY product_id;
```
---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Group all sales records by `product_id`.
2. Add the `quantity` of all sales for each product using `SUM()`.
3. Return the product ID and its total quantity sold.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(single scan through the Sales table with grouping)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores the grouped results for each product)*
