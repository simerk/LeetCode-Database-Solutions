## 🚀 1821. Find Customers With Positive Revenue this Year 🔒

### 📖 Problem

Write a solution to report the customers with **postive revenue** in the year 2021.

Return the result table in **any order**.

---

### 📝 Example

```text
Input: 
Customers table:
+-------------+------+---------+
| customer_id | year | revenue |
+-------------+------+---------+
| 1           | 2018 | 50      |
| 1           | 2021 | 30      |
| 1           | 2020 | 70      |
| 2           | 2021 | -50     |
| 3           | 2018 | 10      |
| 3           | 2016 | 50      |
| 4           | 2021 | 20      |
+-------------+------+---------+
Output: 
+-------------+
| customer_id |
+-------------+
| 1           |
| 4           |
+-------------+
Explanation: 
Customer 1 has revenue equal to 30 in the year 2021.
Customer 2 has revenue equal to -50 in the year 2021.
Customer 3 has no revenue in the year 2021.
Customer 4 has revenue equal to 20 in the year 2021.
Thus only customers 1 and 4 have positive revenue in the year 2021.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

Filter the `Customers` table for records from the year **2021** where the revenue is greater than `0`.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
SELECT customer_id
FROM Customers
WHERE year = 2021
  AND revenue > 0;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. `year = 2021` keeps only the records from 2021.
2. `revenue > 0` keeps only customers with positive revenue.
3. The query returns their `customer_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(scans the customer records)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(1)` *(no additional data structures are required)*
