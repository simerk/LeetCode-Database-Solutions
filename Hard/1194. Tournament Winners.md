## 🚀 1194. Tournament Winners 🔒

### 📖 Problem

The winner in each group is the player who scored the maximum total points within the group. In the case of a tie, the **lowest** `player_id` wins.

Write a solution to find the winner in each group.

Return the result table in **any order**.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Players table:                  Matches table:
+-----------+------------+      +------------+--------------+---------------+-------------+--------------+
| player_id | group_id   |      | match_id   | first_player | second_player | first_score | second_score |
+-----------+------------+      +------------+--------------+---------------+-------------+--------------+
| 15        | 1          |      | 1          | 15           | 45            | 3           | 0            |
| 25        | 1          |      | 2          | 30           | 25            | 1           | 2            |
| 30        | 1          |      | 3          | 30           | 15            | 2           | 0            |
| 45        | 1          |      | 4          | 40           | 20            | 5           | 2            |
| 10        | 2          |      | 5          | 35           | 50            | 1           | 1            |
| 35        | 2          |      +------------+--------------+---------------+-------------+--------------+
| 50        | 2          |
| 20        | 3          |
| 40        | 3          |
+-----------+------------+
Output: 
+-----------+------------+
| group_id  | player_id  |
+-----------+------------+ 
| 1         | 15         |
| 2         | 35         |
| 3         | 40         |
+-----------+------------+
```
---

### 💡 Idea

We need to find the player with the **highest total score in each group**. If there is a tie, the player with the **smallest `player_id`** wins.

First, calculate the total points for every player by adding their scores from both `first_player` and `second_player`. Then rank players within each group by total points descending and `player_id` ascending.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH player_scores AS (
    SELECT p.group_id, p.player_id,
    COALESCE(SUM(
            CASE WHEN m.first_player = p.player_id THEN m.first_score
            WHEN m.second_player = p.player_id THEN m.second_score END),0) AS total_score
    FROM Players p
    LEFT JOIN Matches m ON p.player_id IN (m.first_player, m.second_player)
    GROUP BY p.group_id, p.player_id),
ranked_players AS (
    SELECT group_id, player_id, total_score,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY group_id ORDER BY total_score DESC, player_id ASC) AS rn
    FROM player_scores)
SELECT group_id, player_id FROM ranked_players
WHERE rn = 1;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Join every player with the matches they participated in.
2. Add `first_score` when the player is `first_player`.
3. Add `second_score` when the player is `second_player`.
4. Use `COALESCE` to give players with no matches a score of `0`.
5. Rank players within each group by total score descending.
6. Use the smallest `player_id` to break ties.
7. Keep only the player ranked `1` in each group.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n log n)` *(calculates scores and sorts players within each group)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores player scores and ranked results)*
