## 🚀 1412. Find the Quiet Students in All Exams 🔒

### 📖 Problem

A **quiet student** is the one who took at least one exam and did not score the highest or the lowest score.

Write a solution to report the students `(student_id, student_name)` being quiet in all exams. Do not return the student who has never taken any exam.

Return the result table **ordered** by `student_id`.

---

### 📝 Example
```text
Input: 
Exam table:                                    Student table:
+------------+--------------+-----------+      +-------------+---------------+
| exam_id    | student_id   | score     |      | student_id  | student_name  |
+------------+--------------+-----------+      +-------------+---------------+
| 10         |     1        |    70     |      | 1           | Daniel        |
| 10         |     2        |    80     |      | 2           | Jade          |
| 10         |     3        |    90     |      | 3           | Stella        |
| 20         |     1        |    80     |      | 4           | Jonathan      |
| 30         |     1        |    70     |      | 5           | Will          |
| 30         |     3        |    80     |      +-------------+---------------+
| 30         |     4        |    90     |
| 40         |     1        |    60     |
| 40         |     2        |    70     |
| 40         |     4        |    80     |
+------------+--------------+-----------+
Output: 
+-------------+---------------+
| student_id  | student_name  |
+-------------+---------------+
| 2           | Jade          |
+-------------+---------------+
Explanation: 
For exam 1: Student 1 and 3 hold the lowest and high scores respectively.
For exam 2: Student 1 hold both highest and lowest score.
For exam 3 and 4: Student 1 and 4 hold the lowest and high scores respectively.
Student 2 and 5 have never got the highest or lowest in any of the exams.
Since student 5 is not taking any exam, he is excluded from the result.
So, we only return the information of Student 2.
```
---

### 💡 Idea

For each exam, find the **highest and lowest score**. A student is quiet if they never have a score equal to the maximum or minimum score of any exam they took. We can identify such students by excluding anyone who appears in the highest/lowest score set.

---

### 💻 Solution

```sql
WITH exam_scores AS (
    SELECT exam_id,
    MAX(score) AS max_score,
    MIN(score) AS min_score
    FROM Exam
    GROUP BY exam_id),
not_quiet AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT e.student_id FROM Exam e
    JOIN exam_scores es ON e.exam_id = es.exam_id
    WHERE e.score = es.max_score
       OR e.score = es.min_score)
SELECT s.student_id, s.student_name FROM Student s
JOIN Exam e ON s.student_id = e.student_id
LEFT JOIN not_quiet nq ON s.student_id = nq.student_id
WHERE nq.student_id IS NULL
GROUP BY s.student_id, s.student_name
ORDER BY s.student_id;
```

---

### 🛠️ How It Works

1. Find the maximum and minimum score for every exam.
2. Identify students who achieved either the highest or lowest score in any exam.
3. Join `Student` with `Exam` to ensure the student has taken at least one exam.
4. Exclude students who appear in `not_quiet`.
5. `GROUP BY` removes duplicate rows for students who took multiple exams.
6. Order the result by `student_id`.

---

### ⏱️ Complexity

**Time Complexity:** `O(n)` *(processes the exam records and groups them by exam)*

**Space Complexity:** `O(n)` *(stores exam score ranges and identified non-quiet students)*
